FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does AdRamp change things in my Google Ads account?

Through the official Google Ads API via OAuth. Every action requires your explicit approval: you see what will change and confirm with one click.

Is it safe to let it modify my campaigns?

AdRamp is human-in-the-loop by design. It recommends, you decide, and every action shows projected impact before you approve.

What can I actually do through AdRamp?

Anything you’d do in Google Ads: analyze performance, exclude keywords, shift budgets, pause ads and more.

Is my data private?

Your data is yours. It is never shared with third parties and never used to train models. Revoke access any time.

Does it work with Meta, Shopify or other platforms?

Google Ads today. More platforms are on the way.

What does it cost?
